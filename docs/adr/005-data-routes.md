## Route policy versus actual turn receipt

The route policy is a static closed allowlist. Its D003 typed-frame digest covers the documented
top-level, route, and nested policy leaves; only the self-reference and the explicit `test_vectors`
paths are excluded by exact path matching. It does not stand in for an observed turn.

An actual turn receipt is validated by the contract verifier against independently captured bytes,
role, and source metadata. Its bytes, session lineage, Watari launch attestation, and
route/provider/model/policy origin digests are re-derived from the verifier inputs. All four local
roles (`user`, `assistant`, `tool`, `system`) may be captured. Only a local user turn is primary
evidence; provider output is unverified context and never primary evidence. Provider and connector
ingress do not accept the `user` role.

Connector evidence additionally binds a GET-only read contract, source-policy digest, checkpoint
lineage digest, credential scope, and contract digest. Any method, scope, lineage, route, or
provider mismatch fails closed.
